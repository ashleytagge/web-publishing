Jem and the Holograms

Characters

Jem

Jem is the popstar version of Jerrica Benton. She has pink hair and cute star earrings. She is truly outrageous!

Rio Pacheco

Rio is Jerrica Benton's boyfriend, and the road manager for Jem and the Holograms. Rio accidentally falls for Jem, Jerrica's alter ego, and creates a love triangle between the three of them.

Pizzazz

Pizzazz is the leading member of the punkrock rival band, The Misfits. She is super mean, wealthy, and hates Jem. Pizzazz has ugly green hair.
