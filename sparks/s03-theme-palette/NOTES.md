# NOTES — S03 · Theme Palette

## Intent
This page should be a style tile with an obvious and well execute theme that consists of four accessibility-friendly colors. The mood should be spooky and scary but not halloween. Viewing this page should feel like being stranded in a dark and foggy forest in the middle of the night.

## AI use
I discussed palette ideas with copilot, then it built the page and revised it to meet the done checklist requiremets. I asked
AI to use accessibility-friendly colors and then double-checked
the numbers myself afterwards.


## Accepted / changed / rejected
I changed the initial color scheme it suggested that used green and yellow. I accepted it's second suggestion that used purples and creams.


## Dig deeper
Something I did to dig deeper was find a color I liked with the help of the agent, and then suggest that it decrease the saturation of that color to find another color to use on the site. In that way I applied HSL values in my site.


## Open questions
This was a pretty simple assignment, I think I understand the content. It was helpful to have the Nu html checker and webaim accessibility checker. Those sites answered my questions about whether what I created with the agent was truly accessible or not.
