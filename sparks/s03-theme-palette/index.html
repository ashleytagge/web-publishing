S03 · Theme palette

Midnight Forest

Midnight Forest imagines being stranded among dark trees after nightfall. A charcoal-violet sky and pale fog create a quiet, uneasy atmosphere. Icy blue suggests distant moonlight, while a subdued plum adds depth without leaning on Halloween colors.
