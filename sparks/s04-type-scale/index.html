Why these fonts

Write your own font notes

The reason I chose these fonts was to give the page a storybook quality, which suits my fairy-garden theme without making the type overly decorative. I chose Lora for the headings because its shape looks familiar, friendly and inviting. I chose to use Arial for the body text because it is also familiar and its simplicity helps keep the longer paragraphs easy to read and balance out the more in-your-face headings. The fallback font for headings is Georgia so that headings are still in a similar style if Lora doesn't load, and helvetica for body text. The type colors are one of the ways I chose to convey the theme, but they are not the only way that the theme is conveyed. The title is pink but also very large and designated as H1 in the html. H2 subheadings are blue, green, or purple, and smaller than the title heading. Descriptive text surrounding headers vary in color depending on the section that its inside of, but are always all uppercase letters, smaller than H2 and the body text. Linked text is green and underlined to signify clicking.
