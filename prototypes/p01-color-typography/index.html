P1 · Color + Typography

Two audiences, two visual voices

A travel journal for young couples and Tagge Family Memorial Care need very different first impressions. These pages use color and type to meet those needs while keeping text clear and links easy to recognize.

Fresh air · shared adventures

Tagge Trips

Clear blue recalls open water and far-off places, while white space gives the travel notes a bright, uncluttered feel. Black text makes the stories easy to read, and Manrope keeps the page clean and contemporary for couples planning trips together.

Palette: blue #1E4D6B, white #FFFFFF, and black #111111.

Font: Manrope for headings and body copy, with Arial and sans-serif fallbacks.

Care · guidance · remembrance

Tagge Family Memorial Care

White and cream make a gentle, quiet setting for sensitive information. Black text is clear and direct; gray adds a softer supporting tone for labels and links. Lora’s rounded, book-like letterforms give the page a warm, calm voice suited to guiding families.

Palette: white #FFFFFF, black #1F1F1F, gray #555555, and cream #F5F0E7.

Font: Lora for headings and body copy, with Georgia and serif fallbacks.
