P1 · Color + Typography

Two pages. Two distinct voices.

Design choices shaped around the people each page serves.

For couples who love to travel

Tagge Trips

Using the color blue recalls water and far-away places, so it makes a pretty, on-theme accent color. Intentional whitespace is important between blog posts to keep the trip stories uncluttered. The Manrope font looks friendly and gives the page a contemporary feel. These sleek, modern choices are meant to appeal to young couples planning adventures together.

Palette: blue #1E4D6B, white #FFFFFF, and black #111111.

Font: Manrope for headings and body copy, with Arial and sans-serif fallbacks.

For families arranging a memorial

Tagge Family Memorial Care

This page uses white and cream to create a quiet, peaceful space for sensitive information about users' family and friends. I chose Lora for the main font because it looks book-like and feels warm. I kept the font black so information is straightforward to find. The goal was to provide clear, focused guidance for people requesting help or planning for future care.

Palette: white #FFFFFF, black #1F1F1F, gray #555555, and cream #F5F0E7.

Font: Lora for headings and body copy, with Georgia and serif fallbacks.
