Arizona · Red rock country

A weekend in Arizona

The trip

We spent a long weekend exploring Sedona’s red rock trails and scenic overlooks. Slow mornings, plenty of water, and time to stop for the views made the trip feel relaxed instead of rushed.

Our favorite moment

Watching the rocks change color as the sun went down was the highlight. We found a quiet place to sit, shared a snack, and enjoyed the evening without checking the clock.

Tips for two

  • Start hikes early and bring more water than you expect to need.
  • Choose one trail together and leave room for scenic stops.

California · Disneyland

A day at Disneyland

The trip

We spent the day enjoying the rides, music, and little details throughout the park. Picking a few shared favorites gave us a plan while still leaving time to explore.

Our favorite moment

We ended the evening watching the lights come on around the park. Taking a break together before the nighttime crowds made the day feel special right to the end.

Tips for two

  • Agree on a few must-do attractions before you arrive.
  • Plan quiet breaks and keep water and snacks close by.

Hawaii · Oahu

Island days on Oahu

The trip

We mixed beach time with drives to explore different parts of Oahu. Leaving space between plans helped us enjoy the ocean, try local food, and take in the island at an easy pace.

Our favorite moment

A quiet morning by the water became our favorite memory. We watched the waves, talked about the trip so far, and let the day begin slowly.

Tips for two

  • Check local guidance before swimming or hiking.
  • Respect the land, wildlife, and local communities wherever you visit.
