## Intent
Make a page of cards for someone choosing a christmas movie to watch. This is for anyone who likes classic christmas movies and wants to browse different options. 

## AI use
I used copilot to make a page of cards. I asked it to include a header and a nav, to lay out the cards with flexbox, and add links big enough to tap. I also asked it to use only two or three reused rem size, which are 1rem and 1.5rem.

## Accepted / changed / rejected
I kept most of the plan it presented. It suggested laying out 4 cards but I wanted 6. I had it build it out with 4 cards and then reviewed it to see if I liked it, but it confirmed to me that I wanted 6 so I changed that. I updated the color scheme as well to look more christmas-like.

## Dig deeper
One thing I looked up was how margin-top works in a flex column because in my prototype, it pushes each of the links on a card toward the bottom of that card. That makes the links line up in a nice row across the bottom even when the descriptions have different heights. (https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/margin). I also learned that margins are different than padding because margin put space around an object, and padding puts space inside an object.
